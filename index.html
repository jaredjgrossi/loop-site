Built for 7am, not just for New York. Loop scores every route on the hour you'll run it. Ferry landings and school drop-off mark the esplanade busy at 8:05; the same request at 7 is clear. Closures are hard rules with expiry dates. Lights are counted per mile. Where the data runs out, the card says not scored instead of guessing.

It talks like a runner. A lap of the Reservoir is one lap. Around Prospect Park is the drive loop, and if 3 miles won't cover it, Loop says so and asks.
